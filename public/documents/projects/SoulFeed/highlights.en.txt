Highlight 1: Five AI Character Comment System

After users enter one thing they did today, the app asks five characters to provide comments and scores. Each character has a speaking style, scoring range, and emotional role, so the output feels like a thread of different reactions rather than one generic answer.

Highlight 2: Social-Feed-Like Interaction

The UI uses post cards, character avatars, floating tabs, and result cards, making the flow feel like posting a life update instead of filling out a form. This supports SoulFeed's "inner feed" concept.

Highlight 3: Replying to Characters

Each character can receive follow-up replies. The app preserves the original event, initial comment, and subsequent conversation so replies continue from the previous context.

Highlight 4: Guest Mode and Cloud History After Login

Guest records are saved locally first. After login, records can sync to the user's cloud account, allowing people to try the app before committing to an account.

Highlight 5: Multiple AI Models and Fallbacks

The project integrates Gemini and DeepSeek and keeps model-switching logic. If AI output fails or returns unstable formatting, the app provides fallback comments so users do not hit a broken flow.

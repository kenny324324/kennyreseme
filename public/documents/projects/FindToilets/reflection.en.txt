Reflection & Takeaways

**Solving a real pain point:**The biggest reward was seeing that the app genuinely helped people with the same need I had. Downloads and feedback made the work feel concrete and useful.

**Practical iOS design experience:**Adapting to iOS 26 Liquid Glass helped me understand Apple's design direction more deeply and prepare for future Swift development.

**Data and map performance experience:**Handling government data issues and map marker performance gave me real map-app development experience that cannot be learned from tutorials alone.

**Iterating from feedback:**After launch, user feedback helped me prioritize improvements based on real needs rather than only my own assumptions.

Reflection & Takeaways

**New technical territory:**Pickup pushed me into AI vision recognition, StoreKit 2 subscriptions and IAP, Firebase Cloud Functions scheduling, push notifications, and WidgetKit. Each area was learned and implemented from scratch.

**Motivation from user growth:**Pickup has grown the fastest among my personal apps, with 2,000+ first-time downloads, 3,000+ users, and 4,000+ tracked packages. Seeing people use an idea I built has been very motivating.

**Feedback-driven development:**Positive feedback about multi-carrier integration and the clean interface confirmed the product direction, while suggestions from users continued to drive iteration.

**Product operation mindset:**Building, designing, launching, and monetizing the app helped me understand the business side of a product, especially subscription packaging and pricing.

**Server-side development practice:**Firebase Cloud Functions in TypeScript gave me practical experience with scheduled backend work, push triggers, and AI proxy services.

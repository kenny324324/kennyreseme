Highlight 1: Clear Inventory Overview

The home screen acts as the inventory dashboard, showing real-time warehouse stock through a "today's warehouse inventory" section. Clear product names and prominent quantity values let managers understand stock status at a glance.

Highlight 2: Intuitive Scanning Interface

The scanning screen uses a full-screen scan area with clear guidance for barcode scanning or manual input. Hardware scanning, manual entry, and preset quick options support different operating needs.

Highlight 3: Complete Transaction Document Tracking

The document detail structure organizes document numbers, customer data, timestamps, totals, and product lines with clear hierarchy and status labels, making complex transaction information easier to inspect.

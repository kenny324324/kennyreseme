Reflection & Takeaways

The switch from software scanning to hardware integration taught me .NET MAUI hardware integration APIs and showed how B2B products must choose technology based on the actual operating environment.

In a warehouse with poor lighting, a hardware scanner was far more reliable than a camera-based solution, which made me understand that technical choices cannot be based only on development cost.

Building directly in code instead of starting with a traditional Figma phase taught me how to adjust UI during development and iterate quickly from user feedback.

Weekly demos with on-site staff showed that practical details, such as button size for one-handed operation, can matter more than visual polish in B2B tools.

With limited resources, I learned how to balance development speed and product quality while practicing the core idea of agile iteration.

The TUAN AI Assistant App was commissioned by Hafeice as a voice-based interactive application. The goal was to build an AI conversation platform that combined licensed artist portraits with conversational AI.

The technical scope included speech recognition, natural language processing, voice synthesis that could convert AI text replies into a specific artist-like voice, and image processing that made artist photos appear to speak dynamically.

The project lasted four months and required a cross-platform mobile app, multiple API integrations, and an interface designed around voice interaction.
